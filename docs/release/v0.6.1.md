MeshDrop 0.6.1: restarts itself after an update.

When an update (the .deb, or App Hub) replaced MeshDrop while it was running, its window could show program text instead of the app until MeshDrop was quit and started again. MeshDrop now notices the update and restarts as the new version on its own (staying in the tray if its window was closed).

If 0.6.0 or older shows that text after this update: quit MeshDrop from its tray icon and open it again, once.
