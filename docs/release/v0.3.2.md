MeshDrop 0.3.2: sync more than one folder.

- **More → Sync** now holds as many folders as you like, each with its own devices to sync with.
- Folders match by **name**: give a folder the same name on each device (it starts as the folder's own name; rename it on its card). So "Docs" on the PC syncs with "Docs" on the tablet even if the folders sit in different places.
- **Stop syncing** a folder keeps its files where they are.
- Your existing synced folder carries over (named after its folder), and devices still on 0.3.0/0.3.1 keep syncing with it.
