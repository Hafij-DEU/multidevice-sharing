MeshDrop 0.2.3: a better fit on phones.

- **Tabs at the bottom** on phones (Send, Received, More, with icons), in reach of your thumb.
- **Sizes follow the screen:** text and buttons grow with the display width; buttons are easy to tap.
- **Android:** nothing hides under the status bar, the gesture bar or the camera cutout any more.
- **Clearer errors:** "not paired back yet" (with what to press on the other device) instead of "http-403 untrusted"; "paused"; "did not answer".
