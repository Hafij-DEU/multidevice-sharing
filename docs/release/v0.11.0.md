MeshDrop 0.11.0: the Wi-Fi tab is gone. Saved networks now live in the separate Band Lock app.

- **Wi-Fi tab removed:** MeshDrop no longer saves Wi-Fi networks, locks to a band, adds eduroam / 802.1X networks or syncs networks between devices. That feature moved to the Band Lock app (0.2.0). On Android and on the PC.
- **Clean update:** on its first start, MeshDrop removes the networks it had offered to Android (so no band lock stays pinned), its stored networks and their key. Networks saved in MeshDrop cannot be moved; enter them again in Band Lock.
- **Fewer permissions:** location permission is again asked only on Android 12 and older.
- **Unchanged:** sending files over your Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot, the hotspot QR code and the Wi-Fi / Bluetooth link setting.
- This app was generated with AI. Developer support may not be available.
