MeshDrop 0.9.1: see and share your Wi-Fi networks on the desktop, and more ways to use them on Android.

- **Your Wi-Fi networks on the desktop (Linux and Windows):** the Wi-Fi tab now works in the desktop app. It shows the networks you saved on your phone, synced over to your computer, with their names, security and band details. For each one you can copy the password, show a QR code to scan from another device, or share the full details. The desktop app does not connect your computer to Wi-Fi for you — it gives you the password and QR so you can connect from your own operating system's Wi-Fi settings. Available in both the Linux `.deb` and the Windows installer.
- **Passwords stay out of the window:** the passphrase and any eduroam / 802.1X password are never handed to the on-screen view. They are read only in the background when you copy, show a QR, or share, and the saved file on disk is encrypted with the computer's own key where the system offers one.
- **Sync carries the secrets safely:** "Sync to my devices" moves your saved networks, passwords included, only over the encrypted, pinned link between devices you have already paired. Each device re-encrypts the networks at rest with its own device-bound key, so a readable copy never touches disk.
- **More on Android too:** the Android Wi-Fi tab gains copy password, show QR and share, plus a dedicated band-lock that pins a saved network to one access point without re-checking the password. Saved networks are now auto-detected as WPA3 when the access point advertises it.
- **eduroam and other 802.1X networks** are handled on both: there is no single password to copy, show as a QR, or share, so those actions say so instead of leaking a login.
- **Windows installer:** `MeshDrop-Setup-0.9.1.exe` is attached to this release.
- No features removed.
- This app was generated with AI. Developer support may not be available.
