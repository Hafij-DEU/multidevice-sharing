MeshDrop 0.10.2: Bluetooth headphones on the PC no longer drop while MeshDrop runs.

- **Quiet while you listen:** while the PC plays sound to Bluetooth headphones or a speaker, MeshDrop no longer searches in the background for phones that are away. On some laptops (Intel AX201) each search stalled the sound and then dropped the headphones. A send you start yourself still connects over Bluetooth.
- **Fewer searches:** when a phone does not answer, MeshDrop now waits up to 15 minutes between tries (it was 2 minutes).
- **Quick reconnect:** when a paired phone connects to the PC over Bluetooth for any reason, MeshDrop links to it right away.
- This app was generated with AI. Developer support may not be available.
