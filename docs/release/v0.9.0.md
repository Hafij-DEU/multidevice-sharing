MeshDrop 0.9.0: save your own Wi-Fi networks, band-lock them, and sync them across your devices.

- **Your Wi-Fi networks, inside MeshDrop (Android):** a new Wi-Fi tab where you save the networks you use — type the password, even one you forgot elsewhere and are re-entering here. MeshDrop then offers them to Android as network suggestions so your phone connects on its own. Nothing reads other apps' or the system's saved passwords; Android has no API for that, and MeshDrop only ever stores what you type in.
- **Band lock:** pin a network to one access point so the phone cannot drift onto a slower band. Pick the AP from a scan. Pinning needs Android 11 or newer; below that the network is still saved and suggested, just not pinned. For a lock to win, forget that network in Android's own Wi-Fi settings first — a system-saved network beats MeshDrop's pin.
- **eduroam and other 802.1X networks:** enterprise (WPA2-EAP) profiles with EAP method, inner phase-2, identity, anonymous identity, CA certificate and server-name match. You can paste an eap-config / CAT XML (for example a geteduroam download) to fill in the details.
- **Sync to your own devices:** a "Sync to my devices" button shares your saved networks, passwords and enterprise secrets included, with your other trusted devices, so a synced network actually connects. It travels only the encrypted, pinned link between devices you have paired; each device re-encrypts the networks at rest with its own device-bound key, so a readable copy never touches disk. Deleting a network removes it from your other devices too, and a deleted network will not come back from a device that still had it.
- **All passphrases are stored encrypted** (AES-256-GCM, key held in the Android Keystore) and are never shown back in the app or sent to the PC view.
- **Windows installer:** `MeshDrop-Setup-0.9.0.exe` is attached to this release.
- No features removed.
- This app was generated with AI. Developer support may not be available.
