MeshDrop 0.10.1: two ways to join a Wi-Fi network on Android.

- **Connect through MeshDrop:** MeshDrop adds the network as an app suggestion. Band lock and the fallback network work. Android hides Forget for it in Settings; use "Stop connecting through MeshDrop" in the app instead.
- **Connect directly:** Android saves the network itself through its Save popup. Forget works in Settings, and the network stays after you remove MeshDrop. MeshDrop asks Android to keep the band-lock access point, but some phones may ignore it.
- The choice is kept on each device and is not synced to your other devices. Networks pinned before this release keep connecting through MeshDrop.
- All 10 languages are updated for the new buttons and hints.
- This app was generated with AI. Developer support may not be available.
