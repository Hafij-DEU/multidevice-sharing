MeshDrop 0.5.1: a Home page.

**Home** is the new first tab, on the PC and the phone.
- **At a glance:** your device, how many of your devices are online, how you reach your hub, and your phones' battery.
- **Alerts:** a Stop button while this device rings, and a line for new phone notifications.
- **Every feature one tap away:** Send, Received, Send clipboard, Voice note, Scan to PDF, Remote, Find my device, Guests, Room, Internet links, Sync & backup, Devices, Mesh, Second screen, Transfers and Settings.

On the phone, the Back button now steps back to Home, and leaves the app from Home.
