MeshDrop 0.9.6: Share to Xiaomi, and choose what to send by type.

- **Share to Xiaomi (Android):** send files to, and receive files from, Xiaomi phones that use their built-in sharing app (ShareMe). Turn it on in Settings → Add-ons, then open More → Share to Xiaomi. To receive, MeshDrop shows a code; on their phone open ShareMe → Send, pick files and scan it. To send, open ShareMe → Receive on their phone and scan its code. Received files go to Download/MeshDrop/From Xiaomi. It is off by default and works without internet.
- **Choose by type (Android):** the Send page has a new picker with Recent, Videos, Photos, Audio, Apps, APK files and Files tabs. Apps are sent as installable `.apk` files.
