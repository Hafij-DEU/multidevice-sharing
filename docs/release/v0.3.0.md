MeshDrop 0.3.0: folder sync and photo backup.

**Folder sync** (More → Sync, PC and phone)
- One folder stays the same on your devices whenever they are on the same Wi-Fi. Use a folder made for this (the PC suggests `~/MeshDrop Sync`; on the phone you pick one), not your working folders. Tick each other on both devices.
- Adds, edits and deletes travel both ways. If a file changed on both sides, both versions are kept: the newer keeps the name, the other becomes "(conflict from …)".
- Replaced and deleted files stay 30 days in the folder's `.meshdrop-trash`. Sync never goes through the hub.

**Photo backup** (phone, More → Sync)
- New camera photos and videos go to your PC by themselves while both are on the same Wi-Fi, into `~/Pictures/MeshDrop Backup/<phone>/`. You can also back up the photos already on the phone.

Also: unpairing a device removes it from sync.

Not tested on a real phone yet: the Android folder picker access and camera-roll reading were only tested on the emulator and in unit tests.
