MeshDrop 0.3.1: one QR for all your devices; Back works on Android.

- **One QR for all your devices:** on the PC, Send → Add a device → **Show one QR for all my devices**. Scan it with MeshDrop on each phone or tablet (**Scan QR** is right there on the phone's Send page). Every device joins and sees every other one: no pairing each pair. The QR works for 15 minutes, for up to 20 devices. If there is no mesh yet, the PC becomes its hub.
- **Android Back button:** it now steps back inside the app (a page under More → More → Send) instead of closing it. On the Send page, Back puts MeshDrop in the background; it keeps receiving.
