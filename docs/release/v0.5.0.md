MeshDrop 0.5.0: your phone as a remote, a hub that is always on, and a lot of extras.

**Phone as a remote** (More → Remote)
- Touchpad, keyboard, media and volume keys, and a presenter with next/previous slide and a laser dot on the PC screen.
- PC actions: lock, sleep, shut down, restart, open an app. On the PC, tick the devices that may control it. The first time, GNOME asks once to allow remote interaction.
- **Find my device:** Ring makes the phone ring at full volume, even on silent (or makes the PC ring).
- The PC shows each phone's battery, free space and signal.
- **Phone notifications on the PC:** reply to messages, SMS included, from the PC. On the phone, tick the PC and allow notification access once.
- **Open link on device:** a link opens on the other device's browser, both ways.
- **PC sound on the phone.** The phone's camera and microphone become a webcam and a microphone on the PC, for video calls; no driver needed.
- **Second screen:** a button opens Extendo.

**A hub that is always on**
- A relay hub is a VPS or a second PC that stands in while the hub PC is off. It covers internet sends, files for offline devices and internet links. Set it in Mesh → Relay hubs, or with `meshdrop hub relays`.
- Phones make internet links too. The files are encrypted on the phone; the hub keeps only the encrypted copy.

**Extras**
- Voice notes.
- **Burn after reading:** deleted 5 minutes after it is first opened.
- **Read receipts:** your Sent row shows *Opened*.
- **Print on the PC.**
- **Send later.**
- **Smart inbox:** sorts received files by type, with optional clean-up after N days.
- **Room mode:** one QR, a shared photo board for everyone on the Wi-Fi.
- **Scan to PDF** from the phone.
- Shake the phone to grab the PC's clipboard; flick the file list up to send.

**Phones that stop apps in the background** (Xiaomi/HyperOS): with USB debugging on, run `bash apps/android/scripts/keep-alive-adb.sh`.

Update the hub PC and the relay hub together; older devices keep working with them.
