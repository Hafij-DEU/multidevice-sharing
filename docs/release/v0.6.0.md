MeshDrop 0.6.0: watch parties, and carry on where you left off.

**Watch party** (Home → Watch party, on the PC and the phone)
- Host a room and share its 6-character code (or the link / QR code). Play, pause, jumps and speed stay in step for everybody.
- Plays YouTube links, Google Drive and Dropbox shared links, links to video files, and videos from your PC or phone.
- Friends without MeshDrop join in their browser. On this Wi-Fi the room link works right away; for friends elsewhere, give your hub PC an internet address.
- Chat in the room; the host can lock the controls.

**Continue on another device** (Home → Continue)
- PC browser → phone: on the phone, Continue from PC opens the page your PC browser shows, at the same scroll position, and YouTube at the same second.
- Phone → PC: Share → **Continue on PC** from Chrome, YouTube or any app.
- Needs the **MeshDrop Handoff** browser extension on the PC, once: `meshdrop-extension-0.6.0.zip` below, or App Hub (MeshDrop → Extension), or the PC app's Continue page. Unzip it, then in `brave://extensions` / `chrome://extensions` / `opera://extensions` turn on Developer mode and Load unpacked.
