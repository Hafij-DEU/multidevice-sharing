MeshDrop 0.11.1: text from your phone is ready to paste on the PC.

- **Straight to the clipboard:** text or clipboard you send from the phone now goes onto the PC clipboard at once, even when MeshDrop is hidden in the tray. Paste it anywhere, no need to open MeshDrop and copy it again. This was broken on Ubuntu / GNOME (Wayland), which drops a clipboard change from a window that is not in front.
- **Text sends too:** a plain text send is put on the clipboard like a clipboard send, with a "Copied text from …" notice. Text sent together with files, and burn-after-reading text, are not copied.
- Turn it off with the "Put received clipboard text on my clipboard" setting (Settings), as before.
- This app was generated with AI. Developer support may not be available.
