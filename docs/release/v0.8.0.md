MeshDrop 0.8.0: phone sound, microphone and camera that keep up with you, with Wi-Fi off too.

- **Phone sound on the PC as Bluetooth audio:** "Play phone sound as Bluetooth audio" on the phone makes the PC connect its Bluetooth audio, so the phone plays on the PC the way it plays on earbuds. The PC lists it under Live now, with Stop.
- **This PC's sound on a phone:** "Play this PC's sound there" asks the phone to play it. Over Bluetooth it stays live instead of falling behind.
- **Phone microphone over Bluetooth:** about 60 ms behind instead of about 230 ms, and it no longer takes over the phone's own Bluetooth audio.
- **Warning while the PC searches for Bluetooth devices:** an open Bluetooth settings window keeps the PC searching, which breaks up sound over Bluetooth. MeshDrop now says so while such a stream runs. Close the window to stop the search.
- **Sound quality over Bluetooth:** choose 8, 12, 16, 24 or 32 kbit/s (12 by default), the same choice on the phone and the PC.
- **Phone webcam in more apps:** Chrome, Brave, Zoom and other apps that only list V4L2 cameras now see "MeshDrop camera". The webcam keeps working when an app closes it, and reopens after face unlock on the phone.
- **Smoother live streams over Wi-Fi:** the phone keeps Wi-Fi awake during webcam, microphone, sound and touchpad use, so they no longer arrive in bursts.
- **Touchpad:** pointer speed slider.
- **Fixes:** a paired PC that left the Wi-Fi no longer beats the Bluetooth link. Stuck sound helpers on the PC are stopped.
- Known limits: Bluetooth audio from the phone needs Android 14 or newer for the output picker; older phones open Bluetooth settings. Sound over Bluetooth still breaks up while the PC searches for devices.
- No features removed.
- This app was generated with AI. Developer support may not be available.
