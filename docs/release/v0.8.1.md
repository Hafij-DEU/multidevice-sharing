MeshDrop 0.8.1: watch parties that stay in step and load faster on the same Wi-Fi.

- **Players stay in step:** a member a little behind or ahead now catches up smoothly by playing up to 10% faster or slower, instead of staying up to a second apart. Videos jump only when more than 2 s off. YouTube jumps when more than half a second off and learns how far ahead to land.
- **Same Wi-Fi first:** a room's Wi-Fi link now comes before its internet link everywhere: in the room list, when joining by code and on the phone. Phones on the PC's Wi-Fi or hotspot no longer stall on large videos through the internet link.
- **A QR code for every room link:** the Wi-Fi link gets its own QR code next to the internet one.
- No features removed.
