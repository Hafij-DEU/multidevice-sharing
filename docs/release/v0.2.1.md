MeshDrop 0.2.1: a simpler, colorful app, phone guests, waiting sends, easier pairing.

**Both apps**
- **New look:** three tabs (Send, Received, More), a big Send button, colored device cards, light and dark themes. Add a device sits under Send and shows this device's address.
- **Sends wait for devices that are away.** Send to an offline device and the send waits ("Waiting for device"), then goes out by itself when the device is back on the Wi-Fi or online at your hub. Cancel it under Transfers → Unfinished sends.
- **One-click Pair** on nearby devices. Press Pair on both devices: each side must trust the other.

**Android** (`MeshDrop-0.2.1.apk`)
- **Guests on the phone:** show a QR code so a friend's phone browser on the same Wi-Fi (or your hotspot) can send you files after you accept, and download files you offer. Same page as on the PC.
- **Scan QR** inside the app to join a mesh from the PC's invite.
- The layout fits the phone screen.

Not tested on a real phone yet: the guest server is tested on the JVM, and the APK builds and passes the emulator smoke test in CI.
